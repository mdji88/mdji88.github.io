About

I work on measuring and explaining market and counterparty risk: VaR and expected shortfall, stress testing, sensitivities, and valuation adjustments. I come from an engineering background, moved into finance, and now use Python to turn risk concepts into transparent, testable models. I am also exploring how LLMs and agents can support risk and finance workflows.
